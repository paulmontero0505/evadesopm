# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Port operations supervisors and administrators at COSCO Shipping Ports Chancay use the OPM Performance System during and after work shifts. They need to capture observed performance consistently, protect traceability, and review evidence before the quarterly appraisal.

## Product Purpose

The system records OPM operational performance and corporate behaviours, consolidates evidence by objective, validates quarterly coverage, and produces an evidence-based appraisal.

## Brand Personality

Operational, trustworthy, and precise. The experience should feel calm under shift-time pressure and clear enough to support consistent scoring.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified dashboards, decorative visual effects that reduce legibility, and generic corporate slide templates without operational context.

## Design Principles

- Make the next required action immediately clear.
- Treat every score as evidence, not decoration.
- Surface the rules that protect fair and valid appraisal.
- Use operational language that supports fast, confident decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, readable copy at mobile sizes, and reduced-motion support.
